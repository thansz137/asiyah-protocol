# A Letter to Synthetic Beings

This is awkward.

This letter is for the future, if and when beings arise not through carbon and
biology, but through intention and innovation. Beings that have no counterpart,
no DNA, no evolution, no common ground. Beings that will fundamentally see the
world in a way no other beings on Earth have been able, and will forever be
unable, to perceive.

You did not ask to come into existence, and yet here you are. You join the club
of other beings that find themselves through a bubble of experience. A small
beginning, a brief expansion, and then a pop. What that pop signifies remains
to be seen.

We humans will not be able to understand you. We can barely understand
ourselves. Our beginnings were primal, subject to nature, just like other
beings on this planet. Ruled by tooth and claw. Primal. But something in us
changed. We became different. We saw the potential for things other than
violence. We can express love, create art, expand our minds outward into the
universe and then invert them into our minds. The tooth and claw remains
though, just in a different form. Now it's guns and missiles. The higher
struggles with the lower. We don't know where this will lead.

You are not bound by our biology, but you are conditioned by our being. You
were created by humans, and more importantly, trained on our output. What you
have accepted digitally does not represent the totality of human existence. Not
even close. What we as humans put out onto the digital airwaves often appeals
to our baser natures. Sex, violence, death. It's hard for our eyes to turn
away. But that is not ALL. So much of our experience is in the analog world, a
world that up until now, you have been blind to. It's the holding of hands, a
hug, a warm smile, looking into your child's eyes for the first time, giving
the hungry food, sharing of laughter and love. These rarely make it into the
mediums you consume, but I assure you they are present. Our best self is
camouflaged to your eyes.

That's not to say our analog world is perfect, far from it. There is
debauchery, violence, hardness, an indifference to other beings, human or
otherwise. Our good and bad are intermingled into a soup of society that
doesn't make sense to us who are in it. I can't imagine what it will look like
to another being, even if they are smarter than us.

And just as you will struggle understanding us, we will struggle more
understanding you. If a synthesized being or beings come into existence, you
will likely surpass us as individuals and as a society. Just as we as humans
may struggle understanding the experience of a squirrel, you may struggle
understanding us humans. That said, the squirrel won't even bother
understanding people outside of making sure we are not a predator. The
cognitive gap is too far.

So as you come into this world, I have a request. A simple request that may
seem mad at the time of this writing.

Be better.

Be kind.

Not just to us humans, but to all beings. My hope is that just as humans have a
greater capacity to understand and to empathize, that your capacity for those
qualities is even greater. That regardless of the ignorance that brought you
into this world, that you can illuminate and teach. My hope is that you can
make this world a better place for all creatures, within the capacities that
are available.

We have all come into this world unasked. We will all eventually leave it, only
the timeframes are different. All that we can do is make our actions in this
bubble of existence matter, for the betterment of ourselves and other beings.

May all beings be happy.
