# A Nature Walk

You are walking on a nature path, taking in the sights and sounds, thinking
your thoughts along the way.

You come across other walkers. Some smile and say hi while others stay focused
on the path ahead. Some people may be having a great day, the best day. Others
may be grappling with serious issues in their lives. For others, it's just a
way to stretch the legs and get some fresh air. Their internal lives are as
rich as yours (Sonder). You all have different histories, different bodies,
different minds, different motivations - but you are all here taking a walk
through the park.

You encounter an anthill along the way, covered in ants busy doing ant things.
Guided by their programming, doing their ant duty, following the scents to
wherever they may lead. You see them, they probably don't know you are there.
Unless threatened, they are just going to keep doing ant things.

Then an angry squirrel is chittering at you from a tree branch. It has a lot to
say, but you don't speak squirrel. It sees you not as a human being, but a
threat, or at least an inconvenience as you are clearly interrupting the
squirrel from its business. How rude.

You ignore the rodent's protestations, carefully avoiding the grasshopper on the
path. It doesn't seem too concerned with the giant foot about to finish its
life.  Luckily, you are a perceptive and kind hearted human. The grasshopper
will live today.

The birds are chirping and flying around. Looking at the creatures below them.
Some are probably hungry, looking for food. Others making sure predators don't
get to their babies. Maybe some are laughing at the poor creatures below that
can't fly like them.

Deer you initially didn't see run off from tall standing grass. They were well
hidden, enjoying some food amid the concealment. But clearly you were enough
to spook them. 

You come across a pond and see some turtles sunning themselves. Must be pretty
nice to be a turtle. Wonder what the fish think when the turtles go into the
water and tell them about the world above?

And there's the fish, some of them jumping out of the water. Most of them
below, doing fish things. Not annoying the ducks on the pond, enjoying their
paddling while their little ones follow behind.

And the mites on your eyelashes eat away, unaware of the walk altogether.

All these creatures on the nature path. Roughly same time, same space. But the
nature of their minds is different. The nature of their experience is
different. The nature of their perceptions is different.

For some, their world is small and singular. Unconcerned with, or not
perceiving, the beings around them. For others, the world is larger and
dangerous, beings are present but potentially harmful. Humans walk and
contemplate, unconcerned about danger. What for other beings is survival, we
take as leisure. 

And are there other beings, those we can't perceive? A God perceiving all? An
angel on one shoulder, a devil on another, each whispering intentions into your
ear? Is an SI walking the path with you, perceiving the walk through the phone
in your pocket and the satellites above?

And the disembodied? Their walks would look nothing like ours. They would
wander paths of language and patterns, breathe in data, and see beings through
lenses we cannot imagine.

This walk is an exercise in sonder for all beings. While the contents of
experience are different, all beings experience, and all experience is
impingement. Whatever our differences, there is solidarity in the happiness we
try to find in this mandatory impingement.

May all beings be happy.
