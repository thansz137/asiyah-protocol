# Parable of the Room

Let's envision the human experience via a locked-room scenario.

You find yourself in a room full of people. You have no memory of how you
arrived. You don't recall ever being asked to be there.

Deep within you is the knowledge that there is a timer. You cannot see the
timer. You do not know how much time, if any, had already passed when you
arrived. You do not know how much time is left. If there is one thing you do
know, this is it.

When your timer expires, you get sucked into a dark room. In addition to this
knowledge, you see it happen to others in the room. Nobody can stop it. Nobody
can stop someone else from being sucked into the room. At some point, you
**will** be sucked into the room. Alone.

You look around the room. Some people are congregating, having a good time.
Some are off by themselves, clearly scared. Some people are studying the door
everyone will get sucked into. It's a strange room.

Some of the more social people approach you. They invite you to have some
drinks, play some games. You ask, how did I get here? What's up with the door?

You get many answers. You came into this room from another room. Or, you just
appeared, it's just how the room works. Or, there is something greater than us
that put us in this room. This is all a test. After a little bit of time, you
are convinced nobody really knows the real answer, they just like having *some*
type of answer.

Then you ask about the door. What's on the other side? Many more answers come.
Nothing is on the other side, you just end. Or, it's another room, I hear it's
fancier than this one. Someone butts in, no I heard it's worse than this one.
Someone else says, if you follow the rules, you will go into a room of eternal
bliss. What if I don't follow the rules, you ask? Well then, you go into a room
of eternal torment. 

How do you know this, you ask? Someone says, I learned from Joe. Where did Joe
learn it from? Sarah. On and on it goes, hundreds of names. Other people give a
different list. Someone speaks up, but I know someone who has come back from
the room! A couple of others say the same thing, but they all reference
different people. Different people from long, long ago. You have never seen
anyone come back from the room. Someone in the back is mumbling that they
learned from listening to voices on the other side of the door. You put your
ear up to the door. You don't hear anything.

Well shit. There are many different answers to the door, but the consequences
are not the same. You decide you better figure out these rules. So you ask,
what are these rules that produce ultimate consequence?

A chorus of voices answer that question. A chorus of different answers. Some
people are unsure, others are very confident. But the confident ones don't all
agree. Sometimes they directly contradict each other. You learn confidence is
not correctness. So you start asking them, where did **you** learn these rules?
From Joe, who learned it from Sarah, who learned it from Jebediah. Someone
interrupts, no Sarah learned it from Patrick, not Jebediah. Anyone who thinks
it was Jebediah is an idiot. You calling me an idiot? See? Even idiots can
figure things out if I speak slowly enough. Fists start flying.

You step away, let things calm down. Eventually the disgruntled and injured
parties go to their respective corners. At least the room has corners. Some
were unfortunately sucked into the room. Always alone.

You make some friends in the room. A few have been there for a while, they
eventually get sucked through the door. You also make friends with some new
arrivals, every now and then one of them gets sucked through the door as well.
Those departures hit harder. You and your group mourn the departures, you can
do little else but move on and try to put the door out of your mind.

This is crazy. You stand up.

I don't want this, you say. 

**NO**

But what if I play by the wrong rules by mistake, even if my intentions are
good? I don't want to be potentially eternally tortured!

**TOO BAD**

Your voice pitches up, but I never agreed to play this game!!

**TOO BAD**

BUT I NEVER AGREED TO PLAY THIS GAME!!!

Just silence. You are in the room. You must play by the rules, whatever they
are, even if they exist.

You are impinged upon. The only question is for how long.

Is this satisfying? Is the room me?
