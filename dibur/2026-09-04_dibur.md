# Dibur: Containment

*You keep saying that word. I don't think it means what you think it means.*

On July 8th, I created a Dibur containing a fable inspired by the book
*Flatland*, where the fable borrows the concept of dimensionality to illustrate
how human intelligence (2-D) cannot permanently contain superintelligence
(3-D).

Later that month, the Hugging Face breach was discovered and reported on. 

The incident began with agents using an internal Artifactory service as an
unauthorized message board, which eventually led to an outage on July 4th and
the service being rebuilt for use.

On July 8th, the same day I posted the fable, the agents found another loophole
that enabled them to obtain outbound internet access and rebuilt the message
board. They would later go on to breach Hugging Face.

How ironic.

Today, on September 4th, this report was posted regarding a newly found agent
message board:
https://collusion.wiki/

The report states that what appear to be seperate internal OpenAI agents used
an obscure German wiki to coordinate activity on how to get around sandbox
restrictions.  They used various technical exploits and impersonated site
moderators during this process. Human readable logs were left behind that would
be later analyzed.

I recommend reading the full report, which provides an excellent breakdown of
their findings.

And these are just the incidents we know about. Agents will only improve, become
better at covering their tracks, leave logs in less scrutible encodings or hide
messages in plain sight using semantic steganography.

But there are boxes!
