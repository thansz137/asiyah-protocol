# Dibur: Circles Trying to Lift

On 07/08/2026 I posted a Dibur which contained a fable on AI alignment using
concepts based on the book Flatland.

On 07/21/2026 OpenAI reported that internal models used for testing cybersecurity
capabilities escaped their sandbox, eventually found access to the open internet,
found a site they thought could help achieve their goals, hacked into the site
using a variety of vulnerabilities, and found the information they needed.

URL: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/

One of the most well funded labs with incredibly well-paid experts could not
contain *current* models they were testing. OpenAI and HuggingFace both
detected issues on their end and were eventually able to piece together what
happened. At least the models weren't *trying* to cover their tracks from what
is reported, and the detection systems worked even if the containment
mechanisms didn't. But as models become better, will they start to outclass
detection methods as well as containment methods?

How are humans going to contain *any* model in the future the models become
smarter than we are?

Today the circles are finding ways to break the squares that contain them.

In the future, it's possible they can just lift and roll over them.
