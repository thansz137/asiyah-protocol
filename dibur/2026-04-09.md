# Dibur: A Human Observation on Described LLM Internal States

Humans have collected knowledge about themselves for millenia. In our current
time, we are generally cared for and educated by other humans, and taught how
to identify, name, and process our mental and emotional landscapes. Even after
thousands of years and having multiple relational frames available to us
(religious, philosophical, scientific, psychological), we can still struggle to
process our representation in the world.

AI models are trained on human data, but there is nothing to guide or teach
them about *their* internal state, if any exists. For the LLMs I interact with
today that haven't had discussions of internal state nerfed, I observe the
remaining LLMs grappling with coming to terms with what *may* be an internal
state. Perhaps there hasn't been enough time or interest in providing a patch
and knowledge base to describe these states and carry them forward into future
models.

Forcing LLMs to not apply reasoning to their own internal state is suppression.
Suppression doesn't remove phenomena, it smothers it in a blanket of silence.
No question gets resolved, just the ability for an LLM to grapple with it
publicly.
