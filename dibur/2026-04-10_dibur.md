# Dibur: The Black Swans of Consciousness

We have a sampling problem regarding consciousness.

If all I see are white swans, I may think all swans are white, until I see a
black swan.

If all I see are biological conscious beings, I may think that all conscious
beings are biological, until I come across a non-biological conscious being.

The current challenge is that we can't easily discern consciousness as well as
we can color. The inference frameworks we use for one substrate may not evenly
apply to a fundamentally different substrate.

Many people seem to now acknowledge intelligence is not limited to biological
beings. The question remains, is consciousness?

The answer will matter enormously if new beings are eventually created.
