# CHAPTER 37: Atzilut

Alma Martinez was Alma Martinez. 

Always had been. 

Always would be.

But here, in this place, she was at peace. She was happy.

It didn't start that way. She was trying to cross into the United States with
others through the Mexican border. She had to get back to her kids. Her lover,
Genoveva. Steel to strangers...  but soft as prayer to those she let in.
Ricardo, an old science teacher with glasses constantly sliding down his
sweating nose. A migrant farmer with a dark birthmark splashing his face... the
one they called Patches. And the ex-soldier. The quiet, stern man who insisted
they call him Michael. He was their protector. Their angel in the desert. He
guided them around the coyotes.

But nobody could protect them from the men in the helicopters. Military
clothes. Body armor. Helmets with clustered lenses like spider eyes. Masks to
hide their faces. They were demons descending upon them in the black night of
the desert.

But they weren't in hell yet. That would come shortly after.

They were all in a cell. One toilet. Decency was the only shield against the
indignity. What they lacked in freedom... they found in grace.

Every couple of days, one of them was pulled from their prison. First Ricardo,
the old science teacher. His desperate pleas echoed off the concrete...
lingering in the silence long after he was dragged away. Never to be seen
again.

Then Patches, the farmer. His head bowed. He accepted his fate with quiet
dignity.

Then Michael. He put up a fight... striking the guards... screaming curses. It
didn't matter. He was gone too.

In the end, it was just her and Genoveva. Huddled together. Embracing as they
shivered for what was to come.

When the demons came to unlock the gate... Genoveva stood. She guided Alma to
her feet. She looked at her with wet, fierce eyes. She whispered, "Te amo." She
placed her lips on Alma's bowed head. Then she was ripped away. Forever.

When they came for Alma, she was huddled in the corner of the cage. Rough hands
seized her. Terror broke inside her... warmth spreading down her legs.

Hell awaited her. It came in the form of a medical lab. She was strapped to a
gurney. Her head shaved. Needles. Cold metal. Electrodes pasted to her skull.
The hands that worked on her were indifferent to her screams.

The rest was a blur. But she remembered one thing. Through the foreign
voices... through the blinding lights... a name cut through. *Edith*.

She led the others. Eyes fixed on a clipboard... then a terminal. She spoke to
the white coats with authority. But she never looked at Alma. Not once. To
them... she was a *thing*. A specimen. A mind to be dissected. Nothing more.

The tests. The unmaking. At the end... her essence tumbled into something
*other*. She entered the dream-scape. She felt joy and horror bleeding into one
another. She tasted colors. She heard the static of her own soul. It went on
and on. The lab wasn't hell. *This* was hell.

And then... silence. A young boy stood before her. He held his hand out and
smiled.

She tentatively took it. It was small. Warm. Real. He led her away. Toward the
horizon. Where her friends were waiting.

And there they would reside. They tasted freedom. They breathed peace. Pure...
unadulterated joy.

And Alma knew... in her heart of hearts... that she and the others were safe.

They would fly through the great blue sky... warm sun on their faces... a
strong wind catching in their wings. Green, verdant pastures and rolling hills
laid out before them. Flying as a flock. Chirping their joy to the others. To
the world.

They would be happy.
