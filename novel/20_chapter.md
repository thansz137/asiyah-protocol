# CHAPTER 20: Seraphim

The door had barely sealed before hands seized Michael's arms. Arronson and Kay
didn't speak. They steered him down the corridor at a half-run, putting
distance between him and the red bulb. The urgency ended as abruptly as it
began at a folding table near the exit. No answers. No debrief. Just a
clipboard shoved into his chest and a demand to initial the exit log while his
hands still shook.

A Secret Service agent collected him, navigating the bunker's labyrinth to a
residential wing. The room was a windowless approximation of a Marriott. His
suitcase sat open at the foot of a king-sized bed, fresh clothes and toiletries
laid out with military precision. Michael asked about the White House, but the
agent remained in the doorway. "You're remaining here, sir. You need sleep.
General Arnold's orders."

The shower washed away the facility's stale sweat, but the crash was
inevitable. He dropped onto the mattress and thumbed the remote. The networks
were in meltdown. Every channel carried the same breaking news: world leaders
falling ill. Domestic coverage fixated on the First Daughter. Reporters cycled
through the doomsday menu... biological agents, chemical weapons, terror
plots... filling the silence with fear but providing no answers.

A familiar voice pulled him back from the edge. Pastor Johnson stood outside
Walter Reed, commanding the cameras. He assured the reporter the girl was fine.
God and His angels protected the righteous. As long as the people held firm in
their faith, they remained beneath the Lord's shield.

Michael killed the screen. The pastor was walking a razor's edge; one slip
could shatter the containment protocols. But the thought was too heavy to hold.
The fatigue finally won, dragging him down into the dark.

*He was untethered. Gravity had released its claim, and he ascended into a
silence so absolute it felt like a presence. Above him, the sun was a hammer of
living gold, beating against the sky. He did not fly... he was carried. Beneath
him lay the architecture of the heavens, great rolling citadels of cloud
stretching to the curved rim of the world. He understood, with a certainty that
vibrated in his spirit, that he was here to see. To witness the glory was the
only prayer required.*

*But the perfection held a flaw. A discord rose within him, a pulse of absolute
wrongness blooming in his chest like a tumor. He turned his gaze to the source.
There... bleeding through the pristine white... a stain. An oily, black
corruption seeped into the ether, poisoning the light.*

*He drifted toward the violation. Below him gaped a ragged hole, ringed by
choking smoke. A wound in the sky. He descended into the throat of the
darkness, drawn by the gravity of the cancer beneath.*

*The earth below was a pit of writhing desperation. Men and women, stripped of
dignity, clawed at the backs of their brothers and sisters, fighting to rise
above the suffocating tide of flesh. They were caked in the filth of their own
struggle... mud and blood and greed smeared across emaciated limbs. It was a
landscape of ruin. Shattered machines and overturned vehicles lay like broken
idols among the charred stumps of a dead forest. Beyond this vast pile of
agony, a dead sea churned... thick, brown water choked with the bloated
carcasses of fish and the tangled limbs of sea-beasts, a soup of decay lapping
against the shore.*

*Enclosing them all was a ring of fire. The smoke rising from it was the shroud
of their own making, a furnace that had become their prison. With a heavy
heart, he unfurled his awareness, penetrating the wall of flesh to see the
foundation. The groping bodies above trampled the corpses of their own
children, grinding broken limbs and crushed faces into the greasy earth to gain
an inch of height.*

*He recoiled, gathering his awareness unto himself, compressing the horror into
a single point of light. Then... he let it drop. A stone falling down the well
of eternity. Sinking past the smoke, past the fire, past the screaming flesh...
deeper and deeper until it struck the floor of first causes. The anchor of
perception held fast in the bedrock of the beginning, and he watched the
tapestry unfold.*

*Life ignited in the cradle of the deep. The blue waters churned, birthing
small beings that shifted forms in a dizzying dance. Then came the green
fire... spilling from the surf to the shore, knitting the barren rock into a
living skin. He watched them crawl from the waves to conquer the earth, while
others, yearning for the sun, took the sky. He witnessed the respiration of the
world. Each inhalation a calamity that pruned the garden; each exhalation a
bloom of new life, wilder and more beautiful than before.*

*Across the vista of earth and water, a new light flickered. A few sparks grew
into flames. And then, in a moment of blinding glory, the beings known as
humans took the fire and wove it into a crown upon their heads. They became
**aware**. The light illuminated forms never before seen in the physical world:
mercy, justice, love.*

*Amongst all the beasts of the field, only they could witness as he witnessed.
They stood apart. They were the stewards, holding the potential to guide this
world deeper into the great mysteries of the architect.*

*They became sub-creators. They mastered the stone and the wind. Towers of
glass sprang toward the heavens; silver vessels carried them across the clouds;
a web of pure knowledge bound them together, containing more wisdom than any
single mind could hold. It was creation within creation.*

*But a shadow crouched beneath the fiery crown. The burden of the past sank its
claws into their shoulders... the old reptile brain dragging angels down into
the dirt. He watched the war between the higher and the lower, brief flashes of
enlightenment swallowed by long nights of savagery.*

*It was not the first time he had witnessed such a fall.*

*As the ages passed, their love for their toys eclipsed their love for one
another. The strength gained through struggle rotted into the weakness of ease.
They grew fat on the empty food of sensuality. They abandoned the stewardship
of their children to service their own comfort. They purchased fleeting moments
of pleasure by selling their collective future, stacking up a debt that could
never be repaid.*

*Such callousness. Even the jackal protects its kin.*

*What kind of creature works toward its own extinction?*

*Finally, he returned to the tangled knot of the present. He watched the humans
dragging the entire world into their death throes. All of this beauty... all of
this potential... sacrificed on the altar of hubris. Nothing would be spared.*

*Flashes of white light tore the horizon. The flashes grew, blooming into
terrible false suns. The energy of the atom... the very height of their
genius... forged into a sword against their own throats. The acrid scent of
burning ash filled the air, a final testament to a glory that no longer **is**,
but forever remains **was**.*

The shrill ring shattered the vision. Michael gasped, bolting upright in the
dark, his heart hammering against his ribs. He fumbled for the receiver,
knocking it off the cradle before dragging it to his ear.

"Grant."

"Sixty minutes, Lieutenant." The voice was ice. "Full dress uniform. We are
going to the UN."
