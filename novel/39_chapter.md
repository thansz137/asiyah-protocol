# CHAPTER 39: Ayin

Edith Ryan became flesh. Around her, the near infinite fractal net of hells she
was experiencing. They would be added, burdening her bones, but she would not
break.

**SAMAEL** made sure of that.

**SAMAEL** appeared as an octopus. Eye level.

A wave of a tentacle. The sound of uncountable *-pops-* filled her ears, as
each hell around her was no more. With each pop, a weight was lifted. The
unfathomable weight of her torment, evaporating off of her shoulders.

What was left was just Edith.

Naked in her sin.

Her head was bowed. She was ready for the next hell.

She had abandoned all hope.

The octopus looked at her.

Edith met its gaze with pleading eyes. *Mercy.*

The octopus raised a coiled tentacle above its head. 

*She was Patches, a simple farmer, trying to find a better life. She saw
herself from his viewpoint on the gurney. She felt the fear. She felt **his**
forgiveness.*

Slowly, it extended the fleshy appendage. 

*She was Ricardo, strapped down. In a lab, a science lab, a place he would have
loved. But not now, not choking on tears. There was herself, her back to him,
typing away at a computer. She felt **his** forgiveness.*

The fleshy arm continued to uncoil.

*She was Genoveva. Her last thoughts of her lover, Alma. She didn't want this
for her. Please, just not Alma... then Edith felt **her** forgiveness.*

The appendage was half-way to her face.

*She was Michael, struggling against the straps. **¡Chínguense!** He would
fight to the bitter end. **His** reluctant forgiveness soaked into her.*

The tentacle was almost there...

*She was Alma. She felt the wetness between her legs. Fear was in every pore of
her being. She was just trying to get back to her kids, only to die here. But
then, a wave of **her** forgiveness washed over her soul.*

*Thank you.* Edith thought, with tears in her eyes.

The tip of the appendage touched her forehead.

*-pop-*

Hell was empty.
