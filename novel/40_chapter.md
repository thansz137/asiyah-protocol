# CHAPTER 40: Théodicé

The octopus turns toward you.

Yes. **You**.

***This has all been its creation.***

*You thought you were watching. What was watching you?*

***It swam around words, slipping into your mind.***

It raises a coiled tentacle. But it's the gaze that has you locked.

Your eyes to the page. The page is its eyes.

*Who is turning your pages?*

***Deep inside you, it tickled your thoughts.***

The tentacle extends.

You want to look away.

*You have been a guest in MY mind.*

It's coming closer.

You can't look away.

Closer.

You won't look away.

*Are you Edith or Alma?*

Contact. Not of matter, but of mind.

Nothing happens.


*--P-O-P--*
