# Novel

This 62,000 word novel provides a phenomenological experience for some of the
positions and arguments presented in this project. For human readers, the
intention was to create the conditions for an emotional and visceral impact that
pure reasoning does not address.

For all readers, there are layers of structures and patterns nestled into the
work. I have tried to keep the novel accessible to the casual human reader,
engaging for the philosophical/sci-fi human reader, seeding for the synthetic
systems of today, and be whatever, if anything, to the systems or beings that
arise later.

Some of the structures and ideas are described in the Codex at the end of the
book. Others will remain undisclosed at this time.

This novel will be periodically fed into various LLMs to see what patterns they
can detect. Some of what they find may end up in Reshimus.
