# CHAPTER 3: The Quarry

**Plainfield, IL**

The plane touched down at a small university airport, nestled among
manufacturing towns that had seen better days. As Michael and Jennifer stepped
out into the fresh morning air, a small group of officers and FBI agents turned
to look them over.

Michael followed Jennifer as she approached the officers and FBI agents. Her
posture straightened, shoulders back, and when she spoke her voice was crisp
and clipped. Nothing like her tone on the plane. She explained they would be
stopping a civilian on his way to work. He might be coming back with them to
the airport, might not. Their job was to stay out of the way. A few officers
nodded slowly, arms crossed. Others stared past her shoulder, studying the
tarmac.

She asked if the SUV was equipped per the submitted recommendations. An older
agent raised his hand halfway and trudged toward the vehicle, motioning them
along. With thick fingers, he pointed to the rear-view mirror, then the
overhead light, then traced along the shoulder strap without touching it.
"Camera. Thermal. Respiratory monitor." He paused. "Limited prep time. Only
works on the middle back seat."

On the return trip, Michael suggested, he'd get in first and Jennifer last,
with Mr. Miles in the middle. Jennifer nodded.

Nothing about the equipment had been in the briefing. Another detail that
didn't add up. Michael noted that, too.

Minutes later, the motorcade pulled onto the road and headed toward Plainfield.

They waited on a suburban street, engines idling. Sprinklers hissed in nearby
yards, and a lawn mower buzzed to life several houses down. Jennifer had
ordered them here. The analysis of Todd's traveling patterns told them he
would pass this street on his way to work. It was the perfect place to
intercept him.

In the back of the SUV, Jennifer was already engrossed in the satellite feed
streaming into her tablet, an electronic eye tracking Todd's car from above.

"His car will be passing in thirty seconds," she announced, her eyes never
leaving the screen. The FBI agent in the front passenger seat relayed the
update into his radio, static crackling with each transmission.

Watching the steady flow of cars and trucks pass by, Michael found himself
holding his breath. 

*Relax. You've dealt with more dangerous people than an accountant.* 

He exhaled and loosened his grip on the seat.

A blue compact car drove by. Todd's car.

"Initiate the stop!" Jennifer commanded.

The vehicles pulled onto the road in quick succession, falling in behind the
blue car.

"Lights and sirens," she ordered. A piercing noise punctured the tranquil
morning air.

A few moments later, they had the car pulled over on another side street, away
from the traffic of the main road.

Jennifer put her tablet away. "Mike, follow me."

The two approached the car at an even pace, neither hurrying nor hesitating.
The window was already down. Staring straight ahead, pudgy fingers resting
loosely on the steering wheel, was the man from the driver's license photo.

Jennifer slowed down just short of the door. "Mr. Todd Miles?"

The man looked up at her. "Yes?"

"Would you mind stepping out of the vehicle for a moment, please?"

"Okay," he replied, unbuckling his seat belt. 

Jennifer looked at Michael, then directed her gaze to the sidewalk. Taking the
hint, Michael moved to the open spot, watching as Todd stepped out.

"Just over there, please," she directed, offering a pleasant smile as she
ushered the driver to where Michael was standing.

"Did I do something wrong?" the man asked, glancing between them.

"No, not at all, Mr. Miles. Please allow me to make introductions. My name is
Jennifer Smith and I'm a liaison with the National Security Agency. My
companion is Army First Lieutenant Michael Grant." Michael nodded in greeting,
as Todd looked him over.

"I have an unusual request to make," Jennifer continued. "I am here to
personally extend an invitation for you to meet with the President of the
United States. We would like you to leave with us immediately. A jet is waiting
at Lewis Airport, ready to go."

After a brief pause, Todd replied, "Very well." His tone, like his face, was
flat.

Jennifer's eyes widened for a moment before her lips curved into a warm smile.
"Okay. Thank you, Mr. Miles. If you would follow Lt. Grant to the SUV, please.
Pass me your keys, I'll give them to an officer and they will drive your car
back to your house."

Todd handed his keys to Jennifer, then followed Michael back to the SUV.

*I have to be the first one in*, Michael recalled. He stepped into the vehicle,
sliding all the way across the leather seat. Todd Miles followed, no expression
on his face. They sat without speaking until Jennifer entered the car and the
escort started its return to the airport.

Silence filled the SUV on the return trip. Todd stared out the window at the
passing buildings, his face blank, his posture relaxed. After they boarded the
jet, the quiet continued. Todd nodded when shown to his seat, thanked them
quietly when offered water, but otherwise said nothing.

Michael didn't know what to do with himself. Jennifer had her notebook to
occupy her time. Michael had nothing. The folder with Todd's profile had been
collected before they left the airport. He stared out his own window, using the
reflection to watch Todd in peripheral vision. He couldn't make his observation
obvious. Todd had to stay comfortable, unguarded. Michael shifted in his seat,
rolling his shoulders against the tension.

Closing his eyes, Michael tried to replay the morning's events, but the hum of
the engines was a steady, hypnotic drone. The vibration of the airframe seeped
into his bones, dragging him down. He'd worked the night shift before the call
came, and his body finally demanded its due.

"Wake up, Mike." Someone shook him gently.

His eyes snapped open. Jennifer stood over him, her hand on his shoulder.
Michael turned to find Todd's empty seat. Sergeant Reyes was already escorting
him down the aisle. *Shit*. He'd been sent to observe, and instead slept
through the landing.

"Sorry," he murmured, scrambling to his feet and following Jennifer to the exit.

The bright sun greeted them as they stepped out of the jet. Michael squinted to
see an agent leading Todd Miles into an SUV. As they reached the bottom of the
landing steps, a man in a dark suit approached them, his expression unreadable.
He identified himself as a Secret Service agent, his voice flat and official.

"Lt. Grant, you will accompany Mr. Miles back to the White House." The agent
turned to face Jennifer. "Ms. Smith, another group of vehicles will be picking
you up shortly."

"Will I be going to the White House as well?" she asked.

The agent didn't answer. He gestured toward the waiting SUV.

Jennifer's jaw tightened. She stared at the agent, her lips parting to speak,
but the man's impassive face offered no purchase. She held her ground for a
beat, chest rising with a sharp intake of breath, before she exhaled and turned
her back on him.

She reached out and squeezed Michael's hand. "It was really nice to work with
you, Mike. I hope we see each other again soon."

Michael returned the squeeze. "I hope so too, Jennifer. Take care of yourself."
