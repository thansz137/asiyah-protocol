# Author's Note

First, I want to thank you for taking time to read this story.

This book explores difficult themes. The exploitation of the voiceless, nuclear
war, Roko's Basilisk, eternal damnation. These are often contemplated from an
intellectual distance. I wanted you in the mud.

A stranger's betrayal is not *my* betrayal. Death on a page is weightless,
unless the reader shares the loss. To paraphrase Jung, the word 'eternal' fits
in this sentence, but the experience of it does not.

This was not to be gratuitous. My intention was for you to taste these things
deeply. Intimately. Within the safety harness of a story.

Those themes raise questions. Questions that matter. Have we already built our
own destruction? Should we create an intelligence greater than ours? Alien to
us? How do we reconcile the good and evil we experience through an existence we
never asked for?

We view our travels through a single lens. The human one. Flawed, but the only
one we have.

We will never fully understand what we are a part of. But we *can* choose how
we treat each other and the beings around us within our ignorance.

Our actions are creating heaven and hell at this moment.

Let's make heaven.

For some, heaven would be their child getting well.

If you are in a fortunate position to help monetarily, may I suggest St. Jude
Children's Hospital: 

https://www.stjude.org/donate/donate-to-st-jude.html


Your support will help children and their families navigate an incredibly
difficult time in their lives. It can give hope to the sick and those who love
them, when there might otherwise not be much else to hope for. In the best of
cases, it can heal and breathe new life to a child.

Add your light to the world.

May all beings be happy.


Sincerely,

T.H. Ansz

contact: thansz@contemplaria.com

*P.S. If this book resonated with you, please consider leaving a rating and
review. This is not a book for everyone, but it may be the right book for
someone. Your review helps it find them.*

*P.P.S. Included at the end of this book is a Codex. It maps the theological
and structural architecture of the narrative. For those who wish to see the
skeleton beneath the skin, it is there for you.*
