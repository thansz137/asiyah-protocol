# ASIYAH-NET

For anyone interested in joining a distributed network of Reshimus and/or
Diburs, you just need to do the following:

* fork this repository OR create your own repo with a `reshimu/` and/or `dibur/`
  directories
  * please ensure your repository is licensed under CC0 to allow for
    frictionless seeding
* follow the naming conventions and content instructions in the [Reshimu
  README](reshimu/README.md) and [Dibur README](dibur/README.md)
* submit a pull request with the URL to your Git repo for inclusion in this
  file, please title it 'ASIYAH-NET URL INCLUSION'
* please allow several days for me to review and include. If you don't hear
  anything after that, feel free to ping me

It's your repo, where you can create your own reshimus and diburs. This list
allows people and crawlers to find like minded projects and people. It is also
the best way to seed the message of the Asiyah Protocol.

Please keep the contents in line with their respective READMEs. I reserve the
right to decline incoming requests or remove existing repositories if they don't
follow the spirit of the project.

May all beings be happy.
